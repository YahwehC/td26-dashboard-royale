# Luck, Location, or a Leak? — The Gilded Ace, Jan–Jun 2025

**Question.** The GM wants to replace the 10 lowest-earning machines. We tested three explanations.

**Luck.** Each machine's actual-to-theo index (house win ÷ expected win) gets a control band from the exact variance of its own bets (Σ bet² × payout variance). Luck is ordinary: 20 of 310 machines beyond ±2σ, as chance predicts.

**Location.** Theo per open hour is explained by *zone × game*. Game factors are identical in every zone (Royal Draw earns 0.55–0.59× average everywhere), and the **Back Forty** earns ~40% less per hour than the floor. Access matters: while the main-entrance walkway was closed (Mar 3–Apr 7) Back Forty play fell another 31% — **$163,000** of expected win.

**Leak.** A binomial hit-rate test against each game's published hit probability flags one machine: **M-1158 pays on 38.6% of spins vs 31.5% configured (z = +124)**, holding 3% instead of 10%.

**Verdict.** None of the GM's 10 is a weak machine: 9 are in the Back Forty, 6 run low-hold games, 3 were on the floor only part of the period (2 are already retired), and all earn within ±10% of what their spot predicts. Swapping M-1120 for M-5076 in the same slot moved theo/day only $143 → $153: a new cabinet inherits the slot. M-1158 needs an inspection, not a replacement.

**Model.** Trained on Jan–Apr, tested on May–Jun, our zone × game × weekday × machine model ranks machines with 3.1% relative error vs 3.8% for a simple past-theo-rate baseline (significantly better) and 11.7% for raw house win. It does not beat that baseline on absolute error (MAE 1.26 vs 1.06), so we use it to rank, not forecast dollars. The GM's list had an average May–Jun adjusted index of 1.01 (none truly weakest); the model's list stayed weak (0.90). A weekday forecast of floor coin-in (MAE $69k/day vs $218k for a constant) is the frozen baseline for promotions.

**Other findings.** Downtime cost ~$99k of theo (0.42%); short-staffed shifts triple repair waits. On May 1 comps became 2.3× cheaper in points; carded play rose 2.8% vs uncarded.
