# Video script — ≤ 2:00 (≈ 270 spoken words)

Record the screen of the live dashboard (browser full screen, 1920×1080), voice over. Practise once; keep each beat to its time box.

| Time | Screen (what to click) | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | **Verdict** tab, top. | "The Gilded Ace's GM wants to replace the ten machines that earned the least this year. We asked: is it luck, location, or a leak?" |
| 0:12–0:35 | Scroll to the **GM's bottom-10 table** and the attribution bars. Point at the zone column and the verdict chips. | "Nine of the ten are in the Back Forty, the quietest zone. Six run low-hold games that earn little anywhere. Three were only on the floor part of the time — two are already retired. Adjusted for location, game and open hours, every one earns what its spot predicts. None is a bad machine." |
| 0:35–0:50 | **Machines** tab → luck funnel (orange diamonds inside the band, red X far below), then hover M-1158 in the hit-rate chart. | "The orange ten sit inside the luck funnel. One is different. M-1158 pays on 38.6% of spins; its game is set to 31.5%. Over 674,000 spins that is not luck — it's a leak to inspect." |
| 0:50–1:05 | **Demand** tab → Back Forty vs rest chart with the closure shaded. | "Location is the real story. When the walkway to the Back Forty closed in March, its play fell another third — 163,000 dollars of expected win — and it never fully came back." |
| 1:05–1:25 | **Model** tab → metrics table and decision table. | "We trained on January to April and tested on May and June. Adjusting for zone and game ranks machines with 3% error versus 12% for raw house win. The GM's list was average in May and June; our weakest ten stayed weak." |
| 1:25–1:50 | **Plan** tab → budget bar, then the three action cards. | "So we don't replace the ten. We spend 23,700 dollars of the 100,000: inspect M-1158 for 1,200, new Back Forty signs for 7,500 — they pay back with a 3% lift — and one promotion weekend for 15,000, which past events say returns two to five times its cost, measured against our frozen forecast. We keep 76,300." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Change the period to **Holdout (May–Jun)** and pick **Bank B-33** in the filter bar. | "Every number is recomputed for any period, zone or bank — and every claim links to its evidence. Thanks!" |

Tips: hide bookmarks bar; zoom the browser to 90% if tables are cut; do one dry run with the filters reset (Reset button) before recording.
